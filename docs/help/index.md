---
title: Embed your pictures
description: Put a public picture from Known Art on any website, as a light image that opens into deep zoom when a visitor clicks it.
---

You can show a picture from Known Art on any website, not only on your Known Art site. The page loads it as an ordinary image, so it stays quick and light. When a visitor clicks it, it opens in place into a viewer they can zoom right into and move around.

It takes a small script, `@awkw/iiif-embed`, and a few lines of HTML for each picture. The picture has to be public in Known Art.

- [Embed a picture with deep zoom](iiif-embed.md): add the script, add the picture, and style it to fit your site.
